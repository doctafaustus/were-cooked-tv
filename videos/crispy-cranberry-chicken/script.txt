A forty-dollar restaurant plate? Nope! This is dinner for four for under ten bucks. Quick grocery run: chicken leg quarters, two cans of beans, cranberry sauce, a lemon, garlic, and one adorable little shallot. Eight eighty-four! Add some pantry staples and we're still under ten. We'll break the leg quarters into thighs and drumsticks, because pre-cut chicken costs way more. The drumsticks will roast in the oven while we sear the thighs skin-side down until they're gorgeous. Then we'll slather on a sticky cranberry, soy, and brown sugar glaze. Shallot and butter make a quick pan sauce, and garlicky white beans get blended silkier than my fur. Puree down, chicken on top, a spoonful of sauce, and a little lemon zest, because we're fancy now. Four restaurant plates, under ten bucks!
